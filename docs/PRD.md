# PRD — INNOVA CONSULT LTD.
## Canadian Applied Innovation & Technology Website

> Получен от владельца 01.10.2026 (чат). Task-hub: `innovaconsult-ca-redizain-applied-innovation-prd`. Комментарии агента — `docs/PRD-REVIEW.md`.

**Website:** innovaconsult.ca  
**Repository:** `ctmakc/innovaconsult.ca`  
**Current stack:** Astro + Tailwind CSS  
**Target launch:** before October 7, 2026  
**Primary purpose:** reposition the Canadian company from a consulting/tax/corporate-services business into a credible Canadian applied-innovation and technology company suitable for grant programs, R&D partnerships, public-sector conversations, technology collaborations, commercialization opportunities and enterprise clients.

## 1. Product objective

The current Canadian website does not represent what Innova needs to be in Canada.

The new website must present **INNOVA CONSULT LTD. as an applied innovation company that identifies opportunities, designs technology, builds software, implements AI systems, launches experimental products, and collaborates with companies, researchers, institutions and public-sector organizations.**

The company should not look like:

- a traditional business consultancy;
- a legal/tax firm;
- an AI automation agency;
- a generic software outsourcing shop;
- a startup with one narrow SaaS product;
- a collection of unrelated experimental projects.

It should look like an established organization with substantial international operating experience that has evolved into a technology and innovation company in Canada.

The central message:

> **We identify high-value problems, develop technology around them, and bring the right people, software and partnerships together to turn ideas into deployable solutions.**

AI is a major competency but not the entire company.

## 2. Company positioning

Primary category:

**Applied Innovation & Technology Company**

Secondary positioning:

**AI • Software • R&D • Technology Commercialization • Innovation Partnerships**

Suggested company descriptor:

**INNOVA CONSULT LTD. — Applied Innovation & Technology**

Do not lead with the word “consulting”.

“Innova Consult Ltd.” remains the legal/company name, but the market-facing narrative is **INNOVA**.

> INNOVA is a Canadian applied-innovation company that identifies, develops and deploys technology across AI, software and emerging industries. We combine product development, business-process expertise and cross-sector collaboration to turn complex opportunities into practical systems and scalable products.

### Supporting positioning

> Based in Ottawa and backed by 14+ years of international business and technology experience, INNOVA works with companies, innovators and institutions to discover opportunities, prototype solutions, build digital products and create partnerships around commercialization.

## 24. Definition of done

The MVP is complete when a person who knows nothing about INNOVA can open the homepage and understand within approximately 30 seconds that:

1. INNOVA is a Canadian technology and innovation company.
2. It builds actual software and AI systems.
3. It works beyond generic AI consulting.
4. It explores multiple technology sectors.
5. It has real projects and clearly states their development status.
6. It has 14+ years of business experience behind it.
7. It is interested in R&D and partnerships.
8. It is a credible candidate for Canadian innovation and funding programs.
9. It has a concrete technology product — AI Workflow OS — that can be demonstrated.
10. There is an obvious way to start a partnership/project conversation.

The site fails if the visitor’s conclusion is:

> “This is another AI consulting agency.”

It succeeds if the conclusion is:

> **“This is an experienced technology company that can identify an opportunity, assemble the right expertise, build the solution and turn it into a deployable product or collaborative project.”**
